3. Cookies and storage on your device

We use no advertising or analytics cookies. The website only stores small items that make the signup work:

  • fd-ea-place (browser storage): remembers on your device that you have signed up and your place number, so the form is not shown to you again. It stays until you clear your browser data.
  • fd_wl_key (cookie, 60 days): the same purpose on some versions of the signup block.

These items are strictly necessary for a feature you asked for, so they do not need a separate consent. You can delete them in your browser settings at any time.

4. Who processes the data for us

  • Cloudflare, Inc. hosts the website and the signup database. The database is located in Western Europe. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and bound by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for any transfer outside the EU.
  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France) sends our emails once the email service is switched on. Your email address is then also stored with Brevo in the EU.

Both act only on our instructions under data processing agreements. We may also share data where the law requires it, for example with supervisory authorities.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your email address and signup details until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, and in any case no longer than 12 months after the bond offer described in the prospectus has closed. The scrambled IP code is kept with the signup record for the same period. Emails you send us are kept as long as needed to deal with your request.

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link, or write to hi@fundaro.capital. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Latvian supervisory authority, the Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts inspekcija), Elijas iela 17, Riga, LV-1050, www.dvi.gov.lv, or to the authority in the EU country where you live or work.
